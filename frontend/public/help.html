PowerProbe Help Documentation

A quick operating guide for live battery telemetry, configuration, and historical review.

Getting Started

  • Sign in with your approved account.
  • Open the dashboard to configure the battery profile.
  • Use Run, Pause, and Stop to control a session.

Monitoring

  • Watch live voltage, current, temperature, SOC, SOH, and power.
  • Focus a chart to inspect one metric in detail.
  • Use the global chart-type selector to switch every telemetry chart.

History

  • Move to History Analytics to review completed sessions.
  • Use All or Custom filters to narrow the record list.
  • Export records when needed for reporting or audit trails.
1. Configure the battery Select chemistry, capacity, cell count, and operating profile before starting a run.
2. Start a session Click Run to begin telemetry capture. While the session is active, live readings and exports stay available.
3. Review results Switch to History Analytics to inspect trends from completed sessions and compare against previous runs.

PowerProbe is built for battery telemetry, operational traceability, and practical session review.
